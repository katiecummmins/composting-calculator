About these estimates. Cost figures are planning estimates based on the numbers entered above; actual hauler pricing varies by contract and location. Trash reduction is applied as a percentage of the current trash cost and can be overridden directly.

Emission factor source. Climate impact is calculated using the U.S. EPA's estimate that landfilling one short ton (2,000 lbs) of food waste generates approximately 0.83 metric tons of CO₂e in fugitive methane emissions over its decomposition period — diverting that ton to composting is treated as avoiding those emissions. Source: U.S. EPA, "Quantifying Methane Emissions from Landfilled Food Waste" (Oct. 2023), epa.gov/system/files/documents/2023-10/food-waste-landfill-methane-10-8-23-final_508-compliant.pdf. Equivalency conversions (miles driven, home electricity use, vehicles per year) use EPA's Greenhouse Gas Equivalencies Calculator methodology, epa.gov/energy/greenhouse-gas-equivalencies-calculator-calculations-and-references. Figures are estimates for illustrative purposes, not a certified carbon accounting.
